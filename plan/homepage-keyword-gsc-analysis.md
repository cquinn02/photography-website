## How to read this

- **Impressions** = how many times you appeared in Google results for that query
- **Clicks** = how many people actually clicked through
- **Position** = your average rank (1 = top of page 1, 11–20 = page 2, 21+ = page 3 and beyond)
- **CTR** = click-through rate (clicks ÷ impressions)

For headshot service queries, anything in **position 1–10 (page 1)** drives serious traffic. Position 11–20 drives some. Position 21+ is mostly invisible.
