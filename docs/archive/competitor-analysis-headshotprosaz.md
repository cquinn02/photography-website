## What She's Doing Right

### 1. **Niche Service Pages**
Instead of one generic "services" page, she has dedicated pages for:
- LinkedIn headshots (with statistics: "21x more profile views")
- ERAS/Medical residency (knows AAMC requirements, file formats)
- Actor headshots
- Corporate/team headshots
- Outdoor headshots

**Why it works:** Each page speaks directly to that audience's specific concerns and uses their language. Better SEO + higher conversion.

### 2. **Location-Based SEO Pages**
Six dedicated location pages targeting:
- Scottsdale, Gilbert, Chandler, Mesa, Tempe, Queen Creek

Each page includes:
- Location-specific H1 tags
- Local landmarks and business context
- Schema markup with service areas
- Unique content about that city's business landscape

**Why it works:** Captures "headshot photographer near me" searches for each city.

### 3. **Interactive Tools**
**Team Headshot Calculator** - Lets corporate clients estimate:
- How long their session will take
- Whether they need multiple photographers
- Planning timeline

**Why it works:** Reduces friction, answers questions before they have to call, positions her as the expert.

### 4. **Pricing Psychology**
Three-tier pricing structure:
- **Essentials:** $550 (3 images)
- **Pro:** $800 (6 images) - marked "Popular"
- **Influencer:** $1,500 (12 images + hair/makeup)

Plus "Build Your Own" option at $250 base + $100/image

**Techniques used:**
- Anchoring (high tier makes middle seem reasonable)
- "Popular" badge on middle tier
- À la carte option for control seekers
- Rush pricing ($100 for 24-hour turnaround)

### 5. **Content Marketing / Learning Center**
"Headshot Hub" with 28 blog articles covering:
- What to wear (multiple demographics)
- How to prepare
- Overcoming camera anxiety
- Industry-specific guides

**Why it works:**
- Establishes expertise before booking
- SEO traffic from informational searches
- Answers objections (camera anxiety, what to wear)

### 6. **Social Proof Strategy**
- Dedicated reviews page with 89 Google reviews
- 5.0 star rating displayed prominently
- Reviews aggregated from multiple sources
- Schema markup for rich snippets
- Client logos displayed (19 companies)

### 7. **Personal Brand / About Page**

### 8. **Corporate Client Focus**
