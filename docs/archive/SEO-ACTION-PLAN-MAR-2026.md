The homepage absorbs 85%+ of all impressions. Service pages are invisible because Google sees the homepage as the best match for nearly every keyword.

**Evidence:**
- Homepage ranks for "corporate headshots phoenix" at position 7.8 — but the corporate page is at position 50.8
- Homepage ranks for "business headshots phoenix" — but the business page has only 34 total impressions
- Homepage ranks for "linkedin headshots phoenix" — but the LinkedIn page has only 100 impressions

### Phase 2: Break Homepage Cannibalization (Week 1–2)
**Impact: HIGH | Effort: MEDIUM | Risk: LOW**

Google currently sends nearly all headshot queries to the homepage because the homepage has the strongest internal link signals and the broadest content. We need to tell Google which page should rank for which keywords.

#### 2A. Add targeted internal links from homepage to service pages
Add a clear "Our Services" or "Headshot Services" section on the homepage with links to each service page using keyword-rich anchor text:
- "Corporate headshots" → `/corporate-staff-headshots`
- "Business headshots" → `/phoenix-business-headshots`
- "LinkedIn headshots" → `/linkedin-headshots`
- "Actor headshots" → `/actor-headshots-phoenix`
- "Realtor headshots" → `/realtor-headshots-phoenix`

#### 2B. Strengthen each service page's topical focus
Each service page should have:
- H1 containing its primary keyword (already done on most pages)
- 2–3 internal links to related pages (e.g., corporate → business, linkedin → business)
- Unique, in-depth content (not duplicated from homepage)

#### 2C. Reduce homepage keyword competition with service pages
Consider trimming or restructuring homepage sections that duplicate what service pages cover in depth. The homepage should be a hub, not a competitor to its own service pages.

### Phase 3: Fix Non-Service Page Ranking (Week 2)
**Impact: MEDIUM | Effort: LOW | Risk: LOW**

Stop pricing, contact, and about pages from ranking for headshot keywords they'll never convert on.

#### Actions:
- **Pricing pages:** Ensure title tags focus on "pricing" / "cost" keywords, NOT generic headshot keywords. Add canonical pointing to self. Consider whether these pages need indexing at all — if the goal is to funnel through service pages, `noindex` on pricing pages could consolidate signals.
- **Contact page:** Already redirected from `/contactus` to `/contact-us` — verify redirect is working. Title should focus on "Contact" / "Book" keywords.
- **About page:** Title should focus on photographer identity, not headshot keywords.

### Phase 4: Content Depth on Service Pages (Week 2–3)
**Impact: MEDIUM | Effort: MEDIUM | Risk: LOW**

Google needs more content to confidently rank service pages over the homepage. Currently, service pages may not have enough unique, in-depth content to establish topical authority.

#### Actions for each service page:
- Ensure at least 800-1,200 words of unique content
- Include a clear "What to Expect" section
- Add testimonials or social proof specific to that service type
- Include FAQ section with page-specific questions (most already have this)
- Ensure images have keyword-optimized alt text

### Phase 5: Blog Content Supporting Service Pages (Week 3–4)
**Impact: MEDIUM | Effort: HIGH | Risk: LOW**

Blog posts should link to and support service pages, creating topical clusters.

#### Current blog performance:
Most blog posts have very few impressions and zero clicks. They need better keyword targeting and internal linking to service pages.

#### Priority blog improvements:
1. Each blog post should link to the most relevant service page
2. Consider new blog posts targeting long-tail keywords that link to service pages:
   - "What to wear to corporate headshots" → links to corporate page
   - "How to prepare for LinkedIn headshots" → links to LinkedIn page
   - "Actor headshot tips for casting directors" → links to actor page
