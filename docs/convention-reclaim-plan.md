## CMQ's advantages nobody can copy
1. **139+ five-star Google reviews** — no competitor shows a review count on their event
   pages (spot-check top 3 manually before betting everything on it).
2. Real photographer vs kiosk: directed posing, nobody walks away with a bad shot.
3. 14 years of Valley venues: big resorts in Scottsdale, Tempe, Phoenix, Sedona — named on
   the page with photos.
4. Local + established vs out-of-state fly-ins and no-proof startups.

**Positioning rule:** never present specs in kiosk terms. Don't lead with 12–20/hr next to
their 50–60/hr claims. Frame: "They run a kiosk line. I photograph people — directed poses,
real expressions, about 20 an hour, and nobody walks away with a bad picture."

**Pricing position (recommendation, Cindy can overrule):** do NOT publish full pricing —
that anchors into a commodity war with $900 kiosks. Publish a qualifying floor: "event
packages from $2,500" — repels tire-kickers, signals HeadshotProsAZ tier.
